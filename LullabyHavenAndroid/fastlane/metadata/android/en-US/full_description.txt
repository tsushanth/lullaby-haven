Lullaby Haven is a calm, simple bedtime story app for kids and the grown-ups who read with them.

Browse a library of original bedtime stories, each with a short summary, an age range, and an estimated reading time so you can pick the right story for how much time you have. Tap a story to hear it narrated aloud, or read along with the full text on screen. Playback includes a scrubber so you can jump back to a favorite part or pick up where you left off.

Save the stories your family loves to Favorites so they're easy to find again at bedtime.

Narration is generated server-side and streamed to the app, so voices sound natural rather than robotic. If narration for a story isn't available right when you need it, the app tells you honestly instead of pretending to play something that isn't there.

Lullaby Haven is free to use, with no account required to start listening.

More features, including additional story packs, are on the way.
