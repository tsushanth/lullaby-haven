Lullaby Haven is the ultimate sleep companion for your baby. Soothing sounds, lullabies, and smart timers — all in one beautiful app.

SLEEP SOUNDS THAT WORK
Choose from 50+ carefully crafted sleep sounds including white noise, pink noise, brown noise, nature sounds, heartbeats, shushing, and mechanical sounds. Every sound is science-backed to help your baby fall asleep faster.

MIX YOUR PERFECT SOUNDSCAPE
Layer up to 5 sounds together to create the perfect sleep environment. Combine gentle rain with a heartbeat, or white noise with a lullaby — the possibilities are endless.

LULLABIES READ IN ANY VOICE
Pick from soothing preset voices to have classic lullabies like Twinkle Twinkle Little Star and Brahms' Lullaby read aloud. Or record your own voice and hear lullabies read as you — perfect for when you can't be there at bedtime.

SMART SLEEP TIMER
Set a timer from 15 minutes to all night. The app gently fades the sound as your baby drifts off, so you never have to worry about turning it off.

NIGHT MODE
A beautiful dark screen that won't disturb your baby. Tap to check the timer, then let it dim again automatically.

FAVORITES & ANALYTICS
Save your go-to sounds for quick access. Track sleep sessions and see patterns with built-in sleep analytics.
